# Fruitulator Contributor Licence Agreement

Thank you for contributing to Fruitulator. This agreement says what you
give the project when you send it a contribution. You sign it once, by
posting the sentence the CLA check asks for on your first pull request,
and it covers everything you contribute from then on.

In this agreement, **"the maintainer"** means the person who maintains
Fruitulator and publishes it at <https://github.com/fruitulator/emulator>,
and anyone they pass the project on to. **"Contribution"** means any code,
documentation, artwork or other material you submit to the project, in a
pull request, an issue, a patch or any other way, from the date you sign.

1. **You keep your copyright.** This agreement does not transfer ownership
   of your contribution. You may use your own work however you like.

2. **Copyright licence.** You grant the maintainer a perpetual, worldwide,
   non-exclusive, free, irrevocable licence to use, copy, modify, publish,
   distribute and sublicense your contribution, and to license it to
   others **under any terms the maintainer chooses**, including licences
   other than the one the project uses today.

3. **Patent licence.** You grant the maintainer, and everyone who receives
   Fruitulator from the maintainer, a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive,
   free, irrevocable licence under any patent claims you can license that
   your contribution would otherwise infringe, to make, use, sell and
   distribute it as part of Fruitulator.

4. **It is yours to give.** You confirm that each contribution is your own
   original work, or that you have the right to submit it under this
   agreement. If your employer or anyone else has rights in work you
   create, you confirm that they have agreed to this agreement for your
   contributions, or that it falls outside their rights.

5. **Other people's work.** If a contribution includes anything you did
   not write, you will say so in the pull request, name where it comes
   from and its licence. You will never submit ROMs, layouts, game files
   or anything taken from a game or emulator whose licence does not allow
   it.

6. **No obligation.** The maintainer does not have to use your
   contribution. You do not have to provide support for it. Unless you
   say otherwise in writing, you give your contribution as is, without
   warranty of any kind.

7. **Credit.** The maintainer will credit contributors in the project. You
   may ask to be credited under a name of your choice, or not at all.
