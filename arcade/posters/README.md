# Wall posters

Posters the host can hang on the arcade's walls (arcade/proplist.ts
`POSTERS`, Decorate → Props → Poster). Each is a nineteenth-century or
turn-of-the-century circus or show lithograph that Wikimedia Commons marks
**Public domain** (most are Library of Congress prints), fetched from its
Commons file page, trimmed of its scan margin, resized to 768 px tall and
saved as JPEG at quality 80. Nothing in them names a game, a machine or a
cabinet.

How they were chosen: Commons' categories *Circus posters*, *Barnum & Bailey
posters* and *Magic posters*, portrait-shaped, public domain or CC0; left out
anything after 1929 (less certain status), scans with colour charts, text-only
bills, a dancing-bears poster, and a minstrel-show poster (racist). The Library
of Congress's own site sits behind a bot check and was not reachable.
